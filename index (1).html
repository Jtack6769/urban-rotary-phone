OUT OF THIS WORLD

By: QC Challenger
COMING · 2027

Something is coming. Not a flat wall that says “here, do this.”
An atmosphere. A place you step into.
You can feel it, can’t you?

↓  Keep going — it’s out there  ↓
— A signal, drifting in from far away —
“Everybody’s laughing (Ooh)
Everybody’s happy (Ooh)”
— heard somewhere out in the dark

THREE STOPS ON THE WAY OUT

The third theater isn’t a room. It’s a journey. First the Moon. Then further. Then further still.

THE MOON

Grey dust under your feet. Earth hanging in a black sky, blue and quiet. This is where it starts.

DEEP SPACE

No walls. No ceiling. Just stars in every direction, and a ship that keeps going. The final frontier, Captain.

THE LONG HAUL

Some journeys don’t hurry. Storm, lightning, sparks on the track — and the train keeps rolling anyway.

ON OUR WAY HOME

Two of us, riding nowhere, spending someone’s hard-earned pay. You and me, Sunday driving. Not arriving — on our way back home. We’re on our way home. We’re going home.

SUNDAY DRIVING

Phase one, in which Doris gets her oats. A little car, a winding road through the stars, and nowhere to be but home.

EVERYBODY’S LAUGHING

Everybody’s happy. Out here, under all those stars, the whole crowd is cheering at once.

ONE PLAYER · TWO PLAYER

They’re coming too — built all by themselves, each one its own game. Not here yet. When they’re ready, they’ll be waiting for you.

ONE PLAYER

Just you, the quotes, and the dark. Your game, your pace, your score to chase.

COMING 2027

TWO PLAYER

Two players, one sky. Bring someone with you — the stars are better shared.

COMING 2027

SOMETHING OUT THERE IS WATCHING

Not everything out here wants to be seen clearly. Some things you only catch out of the corner of your eye.
